VIDEO TYPES
  Videos 01-20  SAT Math Challenges (question, 5-second countdown, trap, shortcut)
  Videos 21-35  SAT Tips (three-point lists)
  Videos 36-43  SAT Myths (myth vs truth)
  Videos 44-50  XPM Tutors offer (small groups, schedule, price, support, demo)

POSTING SUGGESTIONS
  LinkedIn: 3 posts a week from your personal profile, in order. Upload the image with the post text.
  Shorts:   1 video a day. Mix types, for example two math challenges, then a tip, then a myth,
            with an offer video every 5 to 7 days.
  The voiceover is built in. If you add a trending sound, keep it at about 10-15% volume.
  Reply to comments in the first hour after posting.
